✦ QuickComment

Five steps. The first two are required; the rest tick themselves off as you use it.
  1. 1Connect your Claude key
    Open platform.claude.com/settings/keys, click Create key. Name it quickcomment, expiration 30 days, linked account you, one workspace. Copy it and paste it here.
  2. 2Tell it who you are
    Needed so it knows when you wrote the post it is replying under. Click the button: your own LinkedIn profile opens in a new tab, the extension reads your name from it and shows a confirmation at the top of that page. Then come back here.
  3. 3Try it on a post
    Under any post's Like / Comment / Repost row there is a ✦ Propose comment ideas pill, right-aligned. Click it. It reads the post, then writes three options. Copy the one you like.
  4. 4Reply to a comment
    Under a comment, click LinkedIn's speech-bubble reply icon. A ✦ Propose reply ideas pill appears under the reply box. Click it. The footer tells you whether it answered as the post's author or as a reader, and the whole thread goes along as context.
  5. 5Teach it
    ▲ and ▼ on any option, then Regenerate: it keeps what you liked, drops what you did not, and remembers across posts. Options in the footer holds size, attitude and style.
